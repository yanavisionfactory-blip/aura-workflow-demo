# AURA — Prototype Description

AURA is an AI workflow automation prototype that turns plain-language requests into cross-tool execution.

You type a task ("Create a weekly campaign report and send it to my team"), confirm Aura's interpretation, and review an editable plan — each step shows what it reads ("Uses: Meta Ads") and what it produces ("Creates: breakdown table"), with approval shields marking anything that sends, posts, or changes data ("You'll review the email before it's sent").

Before running, you preview the actual result — fetched data and the email draft — and approve. During execution, Aura shows Done / Working / Waiting states with an ETA. If something fails it pauses, explains what happened and why, suggests a fix, and lets you retry or skip.

On completion, you see what Aura accomplished — clickable outcomes, deep links into the tools, a breakdown table, and a full activity log. You can schedule it to recur ("Every Friday at 5pm"), name and rerun workflows from history, and get notified on completion or immediately on failure.
