Your accounts, your permissions.

Connect your own Google account to use supported Google Docs, Sheets, and Drive actions in AURA. For example, a workflow can create a document or populate a spreadsheet with content you request.

AURA accesses connected services using the permissions you authorize. Depending on your workflow, it may process authorized account information, file content, and other service data to prepare plans, run requested actions, and display results.
