Privacy Policy

Effective: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kolesnik Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Kolesnik Technologies,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use AURA, including when you connect third-party applications and services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address, workspace or organization identifiers, authentication records, and preferences. When you connect a third-party service, we may receive OAuth access and refresh tokens, connection identifiers, authorized scopes, and profile or account metadata supplied by that service.

AURA may process content and operational data from connected services only as necessary to display information, create plans, execute user-approved workflows, provide requested automation, troubleshoot failures, and maintain audit records. The exact data depends on the service and permissions you authorize.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, secure, maintain, and improve AURA.
  • Authenticate users and maintain authorized connections.
  • Execute workflows and actions requested or approved by users.
  • Detect abuse, investigate errors, and protect users and connected services.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use data obtained from connected services for advertising.

3. Connected services and authorization

You choose which services to connect and which permissions to grant. AURA accesses connected services using the permissions shown during authorization and subject to the permissions of your underlying account. You can revoke a connection through AURA, through the connected provider, or by contacting us.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of your information is governed by those policies.

4. Credentials and security

Connection credentials and OAuth tokens are encrypted in storage and transmitted using industry-standard transport encryption. Access is restricted to systems and personnel that require it to operate and protect the service. No security method is completely risk-free, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may share information with infrastructure, hosting, database, monitoring, security, and other service providers that process information on our behalf under appropriate contractual restrictions. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a corporate transaction.

6. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide AURA, maintain security and auditability, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data type and workspace configuration. Credentials are deleted or rendered unusable when a connection is permanently removed, subject to limited backup and legal-retention periods.

7. Your choices and rights

You may disconnect services, revoke provider authorization, or request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information. Applicable rights vary by location. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

8. International processing

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Children

AURA is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as AURA evolves. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. Material changes may be communicated through the service or other appropriate channels.
